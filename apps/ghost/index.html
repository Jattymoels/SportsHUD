Using this on a run
  • Allow location when the browser asks, and use it outdoors.
  • Keep this page open and the screen on. A phone that locks stops reporting where you are.
  • Swipe on the display for the arrows, tap it for Enter. On the glasses these are the wristband gestures.
  • Your runs are saved on this device only, and each becomes a ghost to race next time.
  • Watch a sample run without going anywhere.
