What is real here

  • The route, your position, the ghost's position and the gap all come from the recordings, through the same engine as the rest of SportsHUD.
  • The street is not real. It is a flat stand-in: a path, posts every 20 m, a horizon.
  • Placing things in the world needs glasses that know where the road is. Today's Ray-Ban Display does not, and its display covers a small square of your view. Switch both controls at the top to see that.
  • Files you choose stay in your browser.
