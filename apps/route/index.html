SportsHUD

Route guide

What your past runs of a route say about it: how far, how much climb, where it turns and where you tend to have to wait. Shown as the glasses would show it before the start, and as cues on the way. No map is used yet, so there is no water or toilets.

Asks OpenStreetMap's public query service, which is sent the area the route covers. Nothing is asked until you press this.

On the glasses

Keys, as on the glasses: Enter switches between the two screens, Left and Right move 50 m, Up and Down jump to the next or last thing on the route.
