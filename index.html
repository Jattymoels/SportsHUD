SportsHUD

Ghost-racing and golf heads-up display for smart glasses. Checkpoint 1 prototypes.

  • Ghost run: race a pace or a past run, live, on your phone. Build 2; a first prototype. Or watch a sample run.
  • Through the glasses: a run from the runner's eyes, with the ghost and distance markers placed in the world. A preview of the direction; desk tool.
  • Replay harness: plays a recording back and shows the live gap to a ghost. Build 1; desk tool.
  • Anchoring probe: the route, its markers and the ghost placed over your phone's camera picture, using GPS and the compass, with a check of how far off the placing is. Build 3; a first prototype. Or try it on a sample run.
  • Route guide. Build 4, not built yet.
  • Golf HUD. Build 5, not built yet.
